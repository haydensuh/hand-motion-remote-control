# Privacy policy

Hand Motion Remote Control uses your camera only to recognize hand gestures on your device. The video is not recorded, stored, or uploaded.

The extension does not collect personal information, browsing history, page content, or location. It does not sell or share user data.

It remembers, for the current browser session only, which tab should receive gestures and which camera window is open. That information stays on your device.

Questions: https://github.com/haydensuh/hand-motion-remote-control/issues
